Why v12 (SeqTCN) wasn't deployed
Source of truth: runs/eval_v12_test/report.md + voice_guard/state.md §"v12 RESULT (2026-09-12 05:05)", commit 3133c04 — "v12 test result — 0.3223 phone EER vs v11 0.4853, but confound gates fail: NOT deployed", and the report's final line: deploy: False.
v12 was the first model above chance on phone audio and beat the deployed reference decisively, but it failed the pre-registered deploy gate, which (fixed in EVAL-PROTOCOL §6 before any model was scored) requires both: pass all confound gates AND beat the reference on the test headline.
The numbers
model	phone-pooled test EER
v9 (MLP)	0.5087
v11 (deployed SeqCNN)	0.4853
v12 (SeqTCN)	0.3223
CIs don't overlap — v12 wins the headline. It also passed the attack-type-head gates for the first time (leave-attack-out balanced acc 0.763 ≥ 0.70; MLAAD tts share 74.3% ≥ 70%).
The reasons it was blocked
1. Primary reason — confound gates FAIL (9 of 14 rows). The gate requires |ρ| ≤ 0.10 between the model's score and each confound feature (pauseRatio, energyVariance, zcrVariance, jitter, shimmer, HNR, pad_fraction) pooled over phone channels. v12 failed: real:zcrVariance, real:jitter, real:hnr_db, and on the fake side energyVariance, zcrVariance (rate ratio 5.13, ρ=0.544), jitter (3.21), shimmer (1.71), hnr_db (2.59), pad_fraction (1.26). The fake side actually got worse than v11. Interpretation recorded in state.md: "v12 detects fakes largely by acoustic texture, not by speaker identity" — the entity-vs-style confound (docs/CRITICAL-entity-vs-style-confound.md), now measured on the fake side. Deploying would mean shipping a model keyed to speaking style, the exact mistake this track exists to prevent. A human speaking in a controlled register gets false-flagged; natural-sounding fakes slip through.
2. Unseen-channel generalization weakness. Seen phone channels 0.2134 vs unseen 0.4015; gsm_2g (harshest codec, most likely real-world) 0.4469. v12 learned the three specific trained channels more than channel-invariant speech.
3. Accent cells still at chance for every model (en_native 0.4695, hi_native 0.5142) — not a v12 regression, but not fixed either.
4. Large train→held-out generalization gap: val EER 0.184 vs held-out 0.322 (~14 pts) — training val isn't a substitute for the held-out split.
5. Policy, not metrics: the decision rule was pre-registered specifically to avoid the earlier v4/v5-style regressions where a "better" model shipped and later proved confounded. v11 stays deployed by default.
Changes made post-v12 that require retraining
Post-v12, the merged branch (7fdfb51 merge into vaani, then bd2a553 on 09-12) landed the post-v12 plan (2026-09-12-post-v12-plan.md) plus upstream work. The changes driving a mandatory retrain (v13):
1. New playback acoustic channel added to the protocol. The root cause of the on-device failure was found to be the acoustic loop: fakes scoring 0.95–0.99 direct drop to ~0.01–0.30 when played through a speaker into the phone mic (reverb/speaker-mic coloration/room noise flatten the model's cues — level ruled out: −24 dB only moves 0.986→0.924). Upstream added a playback TeleChannel recipe (far room RT60 600 ms, pink noise 12 dB, mic clip 0.3, 200–3800 Hz passband, bandlimit.py low/high overrides). v12 never saw playback during training — its recipe only has none + hashed-phone renditions — so it can't possibly pass the new acoustic deploy gate. v13 = v12's recipe plus a third playback rendition on ~50% of training files (hash-selected), with a pre-registered selection objective of pooled phone + acoustic EER.
2. New pre-registered acoustic deploy gates for v13 (written into EVAL-PROTOCOL §6): must beat the deployed reference's playback EER on test (file-bootstrap CI), plus both eval_playback_loop.py gates (fake clean ≥ 0.60, fake loop ≥ 0.50). v12 fails the loop gate today (tts_chattts 0.283 < 0.50).
3. The fake-side confound must be fixed, not just channels added. v13 is explicitly required to "fix the fake-side confound rather than only adding channels" — v12's zcrVariance/jitter/HNR texture-keying must be brought under the gates.
4. Cache invalidation changes force cache/corpus re-preparation. Merging upstream invalidated all feature caches (the feature_version() hash covers all of pipeline.py, stages/*.py, and the whole parsed channels.yaml — the new playback recipe marks everything stale). The plan requires precise recipe-scoped hashing + feature_cache.revalidate() before rebuilding only the genuinely affected units, then building the new playback caches (~14 eval units, half a training-cache build for the new rendition).
5. On-device verification requirement: v13's ONNX must pass the new "Test with audio file" deterministic path and Live Mic Test through a real speaker before shipping.
One nuance: strictly, nothing after the v12 merge has landed yet — v13 hasn't been trained. The post-v12 work so far is the protocol/recipe/eval-infrastructure changes (commits bcda9ac, bd2a553) plus the feature-input widening from the physio track (60→66-d jitter/shimmer/HNR, which v10_physio showed fails its gate: ITW EER regressed 0.2226 vs 0.1538 — confirming the confound is an architecture problem, not a feature problem). But the deployed v11 cannot meet the new playback gates, and v12 can't either — so the next deployable model must be a retrain (v13: v12's recipe + playback augmentation + fake-side confound remediation), gated by all four criteria: confound gates, phone headline, acoustic-group EER, and the playback-loop gates.
